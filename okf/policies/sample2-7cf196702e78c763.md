Examination Procedures

To ensure a fair and orderly examination process, all students must follow the procedures below.

Before the Examination

•
Check the examination schedule, venue, and starting time in advance.

•
Arrive at the examination room at least 15 minutes before the scheduled start time.

•
Bring your valid student identification card and any materials authorized by the instructor.

•
Place bags, mobile phones, smartwatches, and other unauthorized items in the designated 
area before entering the examination room.

During the Examination

•
Follow all instructions given by the invigilator.

•
Write your name and student ID on the examination paper if required.

•
Read all examination instructions carefully before answering.

•
Maintain silence throughout the examination.

•
Do not communicate with other students or attempt to share information.

•
Do not use unauthorized notes, electronic devices, or other prohibited materials.

•
Raise your hand if you need assistance or have a question about the examination procedures.

•
Students are not permitted to leave the examination room during the first 30 minutes or the 
last 15 minutes of the examination unless permitted by the invigilator.

After the Examination

•
Stop writing immediately when instructed by the invigilator.

•
Submit all examination papers, answer sheets, and any required materials before leaving the 
room.

•
Leave the examination room quietly to avoid disturbing other students.

Academic Misconduct

The following actions are considered violations of the examination regulations:

•
Cheating or attempting to cheat.

•
Copying another student's work.

•
Possessing or using unauthorized materials.

•
Using electronic devices without permission.

•
Impersonating another student.

•
Disrupting the examination environment.

<!-- source_page: 2 -->

Students found violating these regulations may face disciplinary action, which may include 
receiving a failing grade for the examination, suspension, or other penalties in accordance with the 
institution's academic policies.
