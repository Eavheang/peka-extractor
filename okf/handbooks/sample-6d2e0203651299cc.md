Student Handbook

Page 1 – Welcome

Welcome to Our Institution

Welcome to our institution! We are pleased to have you as part of our academic community. This 
handbook provides important information about student responsibilities, campus expectations, and 
available support services.

Our goal is to provide a safe, respectful, and engaging learning environment where every student 
has the opportunity to succeed.

Our Mission

To provide quality education while promoting integrity, responsibility, creativity, and lifelong 
learning.

Student Responsibilities

As a student, you are expected to:

•
Attend classes regularly and arrive on time.

•
Complete assignments before deadlines.

•
Respect lecturers, staff, and fellow students.

•
Follow school rules and regulations.

•
Maintain academic honesty.

•
Take care of school property.

Student Rights

Every student has the right to:

•
Learn in a safe environment.

•
Be treated fairly and respectfully.

•
Access academic resources and support services.

•
Participate in school activities and events.

•
Express opinions respectfully.

Page 2 – Academic and Campus Policies

Attendance Policy

Students should attend all scheduled classes. Frequent absences may affect academic performance 
and could result in disciplinary action.

Academic Integrity

Students must complete their own work. The following are considered violations:

•
Cheating during examinations

•
Plagiarism

•
Copying assignments

•
Using unauthorized materials

Violations may result in reduced grades or disciplinary action.

Dress Code

Students should wear clean and appropriate clothing while on campus. Clothing with offensive 
language or images is not permitted.

Campus Conduct

Students are expected to:

•
Respect all members of the campus community.

•
Keep classrooms and common areas clean.

•
Avoid disruptive behavior.

•
Follow safety instructions during emergencies.

•
Report any unsafe situations to staff immediately.

Use of School Facilities

Students may use school facilities responsibly, including:

•
Library

•
Computer laboratories

•
Study rooms

•
Sports facilities
